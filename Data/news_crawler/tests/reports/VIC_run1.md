### 3. Frankfurt court upholds Vingroup's win against DANG Thi Hue over false reports
**Date:** 2026-06-25 | **Category:** Litigation

In June 2026, the Frankfurt Higher Regional Court dismissed all appeals by DANG Thi Hue and upheld an earlier Hanau Regional Court ruling in a case brought by Vingroup (VIC) and billionaire PHAM Nhat Vuong.[1] Ms. DANG was one of 68 individuals and organisations Vingroup sued from September 2025 for publishing false, distorted and fabricated reports about the company and its leaders.[1] The judgment orders Ms. DANG to remove all content and images found to defame Mr. PHAM and to pay Vingroup's court costs and legal fees.[1] Further breaches could bring administrative fines of up to EUR 250,000 per violation or six months to two years' detention.[1] The ruling is final, and Ms. DANG has no right of appeal.[1] Earlier, Mr. LE Trung Khoa was ordered to pay over EUR 2,118 in costs, and the court found some of his allegations about Vingroup unfounded and unproven.[1]
