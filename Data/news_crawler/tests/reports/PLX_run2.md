### 2. Petrolimex posts VND 662 billion net loss in Q1 2026 amid fuel price volatility
**Date:** 2026-05-05 | **Category:** Financial distress

Petrolimex (PLX), Vietnam's largest fuel retailer, reported a net loss of over VND 662 billion for the first quarter of 2026, compared with a profit of over VND 210 billion a year earlier [2]. The loss stems from its fuel business, which was hit by unprecedented volatility in world energy markets after the Middle East conflict escalated in late February [1][2]. At the shareholder meeting on 24 April, Petrolimex's CEO had warned that the company could lose about VND 1,000 billion from fuel trading in the quarter [1]. Import surcharges reached USD 30-37 per barrel, and the cost of importing one 40,000-tonne tanker rose from USD 25-26 million to USD 85-87 million [1]. Petrolimex also kept inventories high and kept importing even at peak prices to secure national energy reserves [1]. Inventory reached a record of nearly VND 29,800 billion at quarter-end, while cost of sales rose sharply and gross profit stayed roughly flat [2].

### 7. Petrolimex profit falls nearly 40% in Q1 2022 despite surging oil prices
**Date:** 2022-05-05 | **Category:** Financial distress

Petrolimex (PLX) reported a sharp drop in profit for the first quarter of 2022 [1]. Consolidated profit after tax was about VND 442 billion, down nearly 40% year on year, even though world oil prices reached record levels [1]. Profit attributable to parent-company shareholders fell almost 63% to about VND 244 billion, and basic earnings per share fell by a third to VND 208 [1]. Gross profit from sales dropped 18% because cost of sales rose 1.8 times [1]. Management attributed the decline mainly to volatility in world oil prices and domestic fuel supply [1]. WTI crude rose from USD 75.88 to USD 102.07 per barrel during the quarter, and the Russia-Ukraine conflict created energy supply problems [1]. Petrolimex, which holds over 48% of domestic fuel distribution, faced heavy demand pressure as prices stayed high [1]. To avoid supply disruptions, the company changed its purchasing plans and bought urgent supplies from other providers at high prices, which reduced its fuel trading gross margin compared with the prior year [1].

### 2. Petrolimex (PLX) reports loss of over VND 1,300 billion in Q4 2014
**Date:** 2015-02-23 | **Category:** Financial distress

Petrolimex (PLX), Vietnam's largest fuel business with nearly 50% market share, reported a fourth-quarter 2014 loss of over VND 1,300 billion, pushing its full-year 2014 result into a loss of over VND 350 billion [1]. The parent company alone lost VND 782.4 billion in the quarter [1]. In an explanation sent to the State Securities Commission, CEO TRAN Van Thinh attributed the loss to sharp, continuous falls in world oil prices that exceeded forecasts [1]. Petrolimex also cited Decree 83, effective 1 November 2014, which bases the reference price on a 15-day average, and said that, together with a 30-day minimum inventory requirement, it led to losses; it also believed most fuel businesses posted losses in the quarter [1]. Financial expenses rose 2.5 times to VND 748 billion, and the company said provisions for subsidiary investments and exchange rate losses contributed to the result [1].
