### 5. Petrolimex plans lower 2024 fuel sales, revenue and profit
**Date:** 2024-04-26 | **Category:** Financial distress

Management attributed the planned decline to an uncertain global economy, intensifying rivalry among major countries, the Russia-Ukraine conflict, Red Sea tensions, high inflation, and tight monetary policy with prolonged interest rate increases [1]. ### 6. Petrolimex (PLX) reports Q2 2022 loss despite surge in oil prices
**Date:** 2022-08-02 | **Category:** Financial distress

Petrolimex (PLX), the largest retail fuel distributor in Vietnam, reported a Q2 2022 after-tax loss of VND 141 billion [1]. For the first half of 2022, after-tax profit fell 87% to VND 302 billion [1]. The company attributed the loss to volatile crude oil prices driven by the Russia-Ukraine conflict and Western sanctions on Russian oil [1]. When world prices surged, Petrolimex imported fuel to offset output shortfalls from a domestic refinery [1]. From July, prices fell sharply, prompting a VND 1,259 billion inventory write-down provision; without it, six-month parent-company pre-tax profit would have been VND 295 billion [1]. Sales expenses also rose by more than VND 2,570 billion, further eroding profit [1]. First-half pre-tax profit reached only 10% of the full-year target of VND 3,060 billion [1].
