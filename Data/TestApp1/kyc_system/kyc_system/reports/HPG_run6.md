### 1. Bulk carrier Aquacharm collides with under-construction wharf at Hoa Phat Dung Quat steel complex
**Date:** 2026-10-18 | **Category:** Incident

The Marshall Islands-flagged bulk carrier Aquacharm, 292 m long with 22 crew, collided with an under-construction section of a wharf at the Hoa Phat Dung Quat steel complex [1]. The vessel was carrying 170,856 tonnes of ore from Australia and had anchored on 14 October [1]. The collision occurred at about 17:45 on 18 October 2026 while the ship was being maneuvered to berth No. 10 [1]. The impact point was the 50 m extension of berths 10 and 11, and the collision tore open the ship's bow and damaged the wharf [1]. The captain and pilot continued maneuvering, and the vessel berthed at berth 10 at 18:30 the same day [1]. The cause of the collision is not stated [1]. According to the company, berth 10 is affected, the other 11 berths remain operational, and production is normal [1]. The Quang Ngai Maritime Administration then required the shipowner to station a tug with at least 5,000 HP azimuth propulsion near the vessel at all times [1].

### 2. Former VTM general director BUI Thanh Binh faces prosecution over losses exceeding VND 494 billion
**Date:** 2026-10 | **Category:** Fraud/Crime

BUI Thanh Binh, former general director of Viet Trung Mineral and Metallurgy Co. Ltd (VTM), has been recommended for prosecution in a case involving Vietnam Steel Corporation (VNS) [1]. Investigators proposed charges of abuse of state assets causing losses, bribery and dereliction of duty, with losses attributed to him exceeding VND 494 billion [1]. The alleged bribes totalled VND 18.4 billion from coke trading, and iron ore trading losses were put at over VND 434.7 billion [1]. Mr. BUI's relatives paid VND 10 billion in remedy, and investigators seized a 141.8 m² apartment in Hanoi [1]. Hoa Phat Hai Duong Steel JSC provided a VietinBank guarantee of over VND 87.88 billion for remedy [1]. The sources do not state the company's relationship to HPG [1].

### 3. Hoa Phat (HPG) warns of impersonation scams using forged documents
**Date:** 2024-08 | **Category:** Fraud/Crime

Multiple individuals and organisations were impersonating Hoa Phat (HPG) to defraud people through fake recruitment offers and investment calls [1]. The fraudsters copied the group's logos, information and brand images to build fake websites, and forged dossiers, signatures and company seals [1]. One fake offer sought investment in a VND 253 billion domestic steel project, promising a 1.32% return after 15 minutes on a VND 2 million minimum [1]. Forged notices bore a counterfeit signature of Chairman TRAN Dinh Long and a red seal [1]. Hoa Phat said it does not solicit investment in any of its projects, has repeatedly issued warnings, and has notarised evidence and coordinated with authorities [1]. The date of the announcement, the perpetrators' identities and their location are not stated [1]. The report appeared in a weekly business roundup published on 18 August 2024 [1].

## Earlier events (1)

### 1. NGUYEN Duc Kien convicted of fraud over sale of pledged Hoa Phat shares
**Date:** 2014-12-03 | **Category:** Fraud/Crime

NGUYEN Duc Kien, Chairman of ACBI, was convicted of fraud over the sale of pledged Hoa Phat Steel shares and appealed at a hearing reported on 3 December 2014 [1]. The court found that Mr. NGUYEN ordered ACBI director TRAN Ngoc Thanh to pledge about 22.5 million Hoa Phat Steel shares to ACB to secure a VND 800 billion bond issue [1]. Although ACB and ACBS had not released the pledge, ACBI sold 20 million of these shares to Hoa Phat Steel Co Ltd for VND 264 billion in 2013 [1]. Hoa Phat Group (HPG) Chairman TRAN Dinh Long said the group was unaware of the pledge until authorities informed him, and it asked them to clarify the matter [1]. Hoa Phat later recovered the full VND 264 billion [1]. At the appeal, Mr. NGUYEN argued that no one had been deceived, while the court questioned whether Hoa Phat Steel had suffered any loss [1].
